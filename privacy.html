Privacy policy

Version française

Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short

  • Your fishing positions are private by default and never appear in shared images.
  • We sell no data and show no ads.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account at any time from the app (My account).

Data we collect

  • Account: email address (or Apple / Google identifier if you sign in that way), nickname, language, main coast, sharing preferences.
  • Catches: photo, date and time, GPS position, species, measurements, logbook details, note, conditions (weather, tide, moon).
  • Social: groups, communities, members, reactions, comments, reports and blocked people.
  • Technical: device notification token, AI identification counter, error reports you send.

How we use it

  • To run the app: logbook, collection, backup, groups, notifications you turn on.
  • To identify the species and estimate size from the photo, when you ask.
  • To show fishing conditions (using a position rounded to about 10 km).

Sharing with friends

Members of your groups see your shared catches at the level you choose: Private (no position), Zone (position blurred to about 2 km by the server) or Precise. Named spots stay on your phone only.

Communities

A community brings together anglers who may not know each other. Only the catches you choose to post there, one by one, are visible to its members: nickname, species, photo, size, weight and date. No location is ever sent there, whatever your sharing level. Community admins see requests to join and reports about their community. You can block someone: you no longer see each other’s posts or comments.

Service providers

  • Supabase: hosting of the account, data and photos (servers in the European Union).
  • Anthropic: photo analysis to identify the species or estimate size, only when you ask. The photo is sent resized, without position or date.
  • Resend: sign-in codes by email.
  • Open-Meteo: weather and tides, from a rounded position (about 10 km).
  • Expo: notification delivery.
  • Apple, Google: sign-in if you choose these options; App Store for subscriptions.

Retention and your rights

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. From the app (My account) you can download all your data and delete your account at any time: the account, catches, photos and groups you created are then erased.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port and object. You may also contact the French data protection authority (CNIL).
